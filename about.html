About EggVault.gg

EggVault.gg is a free community hub built for Steal An Egg players worldwide. Our mission is to provide accurate pet base income data, verified mutation multipliers, a fast Win/Fair/Lose (WFL) trade calculator, and a scam-free P2P in-game trading board.

Data Sources & Attribution

Live player counts and game icons are fetched from the official Roblox Games API. Pet encyclopedia entries are compiled from the Steal An Egg Wiki under the CC-BY-SA license.
